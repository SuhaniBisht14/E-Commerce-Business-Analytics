# Dashboard Planning - Week 2

## Dashboard Page 1: Executive Overview

Purpose:
Provide a high-level summary of business performance.

KPIs:

* Total Revenue
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Total Sellers
* Average Review Score

Visuals:

* KPI Cards
* Revenue Trend Chart
* Order Status Summary

---

## Dashboard Page 2: Sales Analysis

Purpose:
Analyze sales performance and product demand.

KPIs:

* Revenue by Product
* Top Selling Products
* Revenue by Category

Visuals:

* Bar Charts
* Revenue Trend
* Top Products Table

---

## Dashboard Page 3: Customer Insights

Purpose:
Understand customer distribution and purchasing behavior.

KPIs:

* Customers by State
* Orders by State
* Customer Distribution

Visuals:

* Map Visualization
* Bar Charts
* Customer Summary Cards

---

## Dashboard Page 4: Seller Performance

Purpose:
Evaluate seller contributions and performance.

KPIs:

* Top Sellers
* Orders per Seller
* Revenue per Seller

Visuals:

* Ranking Table
* Bar Charts
* Seller Performance Summary

---

## Dashboard Page 5: Delivery and Reviews

Purpose:
Monitor delivery performance and customer satisfaction.

KPIs:

* Delivered Orders
* Cancelled Orders
* Average Review Score

Visuals:

* Pie Chart for Order Status
* Review Score Distribution
* Delivery Performance Metrics

---

## Expected Outcome

The dashboard will provide business insights into sales performance, customer behavior, seller contribution, and delivery efficiency using the Olist E-Commerce dataset.
