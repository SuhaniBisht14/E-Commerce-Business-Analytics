## Key Findings (Week 3 Dashboard Analysis)

1. Most revenue is generated from high-performing product categories (especially top category shown in Sales & Category Analysis page).  
Evidence: Page 3 - Sales & Category Analysis shows highest revenue contribution from top product category.

2. Customer reviews are mostly positive, indicating overall good customer satisfaction levels.  
Evidence: Page 4 - Customer Reviews page shows majority positive ratings.

3. Delivery operations page shows that certain regions experience higher delivery delays compared to others.  
Evidence: Page 5 - Delivery Operations page highlights higher delivery time in specific regions.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most frequently used payment method across all transactions.  
Evidence: Page 7 - Payment Behavior page shows COD as the dominant payment method.

5. A small group of sellers contributes to a large portion of total sales, showing seller concentration.  
Evidence: Page 6 - Seller & Geography page shows majority sales contribution from few sellers.

6. Some products have high sales but relatively lower ratings, indicating quality or expectation gaps.  
Evidence: Page 4 - Customer Reviews page shows mismatch between sales volume and ratings.

7. Metropolitan cities generate significantly higher order volume compared to smaller regions.  
Evidence: Page 6 - Seller & Geography page shows higher sales concentration in metro cities.

8. Order activity is higher during weekends compared to weekdays, showing customer purchase behavior patterns.  
Evidence: Page 1 - Executive Overview page shows higher order volume during weekends.

9. Certain categories show higher return or complaint rates based on customer review analysis.  
Evidence: Page 4 - Customer Reviews page shows higher complaints in specific categories.

10. New customers contribute less revenue compared to repeat customers, indicating importance of customer retention.  
Evidence: Page 1 & Page 4 - Overview and Customer Analysis pages show higher contribution from repeat customers.

11. Delivery performance varies significantly across different seller regions.  
Evidence: Page 5 - Delivery Operations and Page 6 - Seller & Geography show regional variation in delivery performance.

12. Payment behavior shows increasing trend toward digital payments, though COD still dominates.  
Evidence: Page 7 - Payment Behavior page shows COD dominance with growing digital payment usage.
